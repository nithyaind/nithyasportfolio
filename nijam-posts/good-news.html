The Hunt for Good News

By Nithya Sunkara Indlamuri August 2026 6 min read

Today, I received my weekly magic email. It's magic because it makes me stop. Every Thursday, like clockwork, I pause and think, a rarity nowadays. Today, my magical email reminded me that bad news is always louder. But, the good news is still there, a whisper at times, but there.

Recently, I have been rather pessimistic about the future of journalism and technology. What is journalism? Does it even exist? What am I to technology? Is Alexa spying on me? Am I the writer or a character? A citizen or an audience?

With each day, it feels like we are sinking into a dystopia. Reading the news (or watching CNN 10) used to be a civic duty, but now feels like the morbid equivalent to waking up on the wrong side of the bed #bringbackCarlAzuz.

However, even in dystopia, there is always at least one person somewhere in the world who is having a good day: a friend who got a job, a college student going home on a Friday, a diabetic grandmother who got sugar-free ice cream, a sister who got her film roll developed, and a dad who is one step closer to his dream project. This is my silver lining.

Here is my silver lining report of the day:

  1. 1. The Delegated and Implementing Acts was passed by the EU banning businesses from destructing unsold apparel, clothing accessories and footwear. The Commission says the new move will help cut waste and reduce the estimated 5.6 million tons of carbon emissions generated by the 4-9% percent of unsold textiles in Europe.
  2. 2. Bridgerton Season 4 did not disappoint. But, if every Bridgerton sibling and their partner don't make an appearance at Hyacinth's wedding, we're gonna have a problem.
  3. 3. The FDA has approved Awiqli, Novo Nordisk's new insulin injection for Type 2 diabetes, which allows for weekly injections rather than daily. Approximately over 34 million American adults are living with Type 2 diabetes.
  4. 4. Rhinos are reintroduced into a protected area in Uganda where they have been extinct since 1983.
  5. 5. I ate thai fried rice today.

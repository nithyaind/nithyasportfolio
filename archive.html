Philosophy

Building things that feel human

I care about the ethics of what we build. Technology shapes whose stories get told, whose voices get amplified, and who gets left behind. I want to understand those systems deeply enough to challenge them — and build better alternatives. Whether it's a hackathon prototype, a production website, or a VR training tool, every project starts with a question: who does this serve?
